THE TERMINAL PRICE REACHED THE SCORE AND NOT THE POLICY
=========================================================

Measured on 2026-10-20, on the rewardcheck gate's own episodes, with the fix
reverted and then restored. Both runs are in this directory.

BEFORE - SPDEnv.checkFloorLimits() ended the episode and dropped
terminate()'s return value. See before.txt.

  the last step the learner was given paid -0.52, and the turn limit's price
  is -2000.00.

  the last step the learner was given paid -0.52, and the stall's price is
  -4500.00.

  A stall ended an episode for the price of half a turn, and a turn limit
  ended one for the same. Dying cost 2500 and did reach the policy, because
  the death path accumulates terminate()'s value. The ledger charged the run
  -4500 and -2000 and the run's score said so; the advantage - the number the
  gradient is built from - never saw either.

AFTER - checkFloorLimits() returns the price and settle() accumulates it.
See after.txt.

  the turn limit's price reaches the learner: the last step paid -2000.52
  over 5 step(s), and the score and the learner agree to -1.00

  the stall's price reaches the learner: the last step paid -4500.52 over
  5 step(s), and the score and the learner agree to -1.00

  The -1.00 is the turn the episode's settling cost, which the ledger charges
  and the learner is not told about. It is a whole turn against an episode
  priced in thousands.

WHAT THIS DOES NOT SAY

  It does not say the policy now descends. The gate says the signal carries
  the price; whether the policy responds to it is a run, and TODO.md section 1
  carries the run.

  It does not change any number the corpus verifies. The recorded reward is
  written and read but never compared, and the viewer does not read it.


THE SHORT CYCLE
===============

  ./train.ps1 -Quick

  3 generations, 4 workers, 1 episode each, 4 update threads, everything
  else the shipped configuration. 57.1s end to end including the gradle
  startup, 14s a generation, against 21s-64s a generation for the 30-generation
  runs in this repository's evidence.

  The CSV and the log of that run are out/evidence/short-cycle/quick-cycle.csv
  and quick-cycle.log. It answers one question - what is the policy doing right
  now: how it ends and how deep it gets - and it does not answer whether the
  policy is learning, because the noise floor is about 100 points over 15
  generations.
