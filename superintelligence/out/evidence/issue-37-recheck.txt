## what is narrowed, and what is not

  Narrowed, on measurement: the leak is not the guide page. It is not the
  verification either - two verifications of one file agree exactly. paritycheck
  runs 32 recordings over 32 episode boundaries in one process and is green, so an
  episode-to-episode leak that the scripted policy reaches is excluded.

  Not narrowed: the worker's own recording-side trace. replay.sh compares two
  verifications, and both verifications of a given file agree, so the difference
  lives in the process that made the recording and no instrument this repository
  has yet reads across a process boundary. review.md Plan 1.1 steps 3 and 4 are that
  instrument: enable RandomTrace attribution inside the worker and dump the
  per-episode site and generator logs, then diff them against a verification of the
  same file.
